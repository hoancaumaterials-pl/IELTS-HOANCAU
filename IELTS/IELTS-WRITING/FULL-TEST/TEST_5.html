TASK 1 – OUTLINE THAM KHẢO

Task type: Comparative table
Data pattern: Dynamic
Time period: 1977–2007
Unit: Not stated
Suggested tense: Past simple
Task: The table compares the average annual distance travelled by adults using seven different modes of transport in 1977 and 2007.

1. Task Analysis

ElementAnalysis
Visual typeA table showing changes between two years.
Main subjectAdults’ average annual travel distance by transport type.
CategoriesWalking, bicycle, car, motorcycle, bus, train and taxi.
Time and unit1977 and 2007; unit not stated.

Grouping strategyIncreasing modes versus decreasing modes.
Main cautionDo not assume the figures are miles or kilometres because no unit is given.

3. Writing Skeleton

Introduction — one sentence

  • Original subject: Average annual distance travelled by adults using different forms of transport.
  • Paraphrased introduction: The table compares the average distance travelled annually by adults across seven modes of transport in 1977 and 2007.

Overview — two sentences

Option A — Recommended

Overall, cars were by far the most widely used means of transport in both years, and their figure increased substantially. Taxi and train travel also rose, whereas the distances covered by the other four modes declined.

Option B — Alternative

Overall, travel by car became increasingly dominant over the period, while taxis recorded the sharpest proportional growth. By contrast, walking, cycling, motorcycling and bus travel all became less common.

Best choice: Option A
Reason: It presents the dominant category and clearly divides the modes into rising and falling groups.

Body Paragraph 1

ComponentOutline
GroupCar, train and taxi—the three increasing categories.
Opening comparisonCar travel increased much more dramatically than the other rising modes.
Detail 1The car figure more than doubled from 3,500 to 7,100.
Detail 2Train travel rose modestly by 100, from 900 to 1,000.
Detail 3Taxi distance quadrupled from 200 to 800, overtaking walking and bus travel.
Useful structureWhile X rose slightly from… to…, Y increased far more substantially, reaching…

Body Paragraph 2

ComponentOutline
GroupWalking, bicycle, motorcycle and bus—the declining categories.
Opening comparisonBus travel saw the largest fall, while bicycle and motorcycle figures changed only slightly.
Detail 1Bus distance dropped by 300, from 800 to 500.
Detail 2Walking declined from 400 to 300.
Detail 3Bicycle and motorcycle travel fell from 100 to 80 and 90 respectively.
Useful structureBy contrast, X decreased from… to…, whereas the figures for Y and Z fell only marginally.

TASK 2 – OUTLINE THAM KHẢO

Essay type: Two-Part / Double-Question Essay
Band target: 6.5–8.0
Topic area: Business and Marketing
Task: Companies use a variety of methods to improve the sales of their products. What are those methods? Which is the most effective method?

1. Task Analysis

ElementDetail
Essay typeA two-part essay requiring a description of sales methods followed by an evaluation of the most effective one.
Main topicStrategies used by businesses to increase product sales.
Keywordscompanies, variety of methods, improve sales, products, most effective
Task focusExplain several common sales strategies and justify why one method produces the best results.
Required positionA clear judgment must identify one method as the most effective and explain why it outperforms the alternatives.
Plain-English meaningHow can businesses persuade more people to buy their products, and which strategy works best?

2. Skeleton

Introduction — 50–70 words

  • General statement: In competitive markets, companies must continuously attract new consumers while retaining existing customers.
  • Paraphrased task: Businesses employ methods such as advertising, price promotions, product improvement and customer-service programmes to increase demand.
  • Thesis: Although discounts, product innovation and loyalty schemes can all stimulate sales, targeted digital advertising is generally the most effective method because it enables companies to reach suitable consumers directly, measure their responses and adjust campaigns efficiently.

Body Paragraph 1 — Common Methods Used to Increase Sales

PartFunctionSentence / Idea
TSTopic sentenceCompanies commonly combine promotional, pricing and product-based strategies to encourage consumers to make purchases.
Expl1Explanation 1Advertising through television, social media and online platforms increases brand awareness and informs potential buyers about a product’s benefits.
ExampleConcrete exampleA small clothing company may use short social-media videos to introduce a new collection to younger customers.
Expl2Explanation 2Temporary discounts, free samples and product bundles can create urgency, while improved quality or packaging can distinguish a product from its competitors.
Further dev.DepthLoyalty programmes and reliable after-sales service may also generate repeat purchases by strengthening customers’ trust in a brand.
LinkParagraph linkThese methods can support sales, although their effectiveness varies according to cost, audience and long-term impact.

Body Paragraph 2 — Targeted Digital Advertising as the Most Effective Method

PartFunctionSentence / Idea
TSTopic sentenceOf these strategies, targeted digital advertising is the most effective because it combines broad reach with precise audience selection.
Expl1Explanation 1Online platforms allow businesses to display advertisements to consumers according to their interests, location, age and previous purchasing behaviour.
ExampleConcrete exampleA sportswear retailer can promote running shoes specifically to people who regularly search for fitness products rather than advertising to the general public.
Expl2Explanation 2Companies can immediately monitor clicks, enquiries and purchases, enabling them to improve unsuccessful advertisements and allocate their budgets more efficiently.
Further dev.DepthDiscounts may create only short-term demand and reduce profit margins, whereas targeted campaigns can develop brand recognition and customer relationships over a longer period.
LinkParagraph linkTherefore, its measurable results, adaptability and relatively efficient use of resources make digital advertising the strongest overall approach.

Conclusion — 40–55 words

  • Restated thesis: Companies can raise sales through advertising, promotional pricing, product development and customer-retention programmes.
  • Summary: While each method offers particular benefits, targeted digital advertising provides the best combination of accuracy, flexibility and measurable performance.
  • Final thought: Businesses should therefore integrate it with high-quality products and dependable customer service.
