TASK 1 – OUTLINE THAM KHẢO

Task type: Grouped bar chart
Data pattern: Dynamic
Time period: 2005–2008
Unit: Million tonnes
Suggested tense: Past simple

Task: The bar chart compares the average amount of waste disposed of through landfill, burning and dumping at sea in one European country from 2005 to 2008.

1. Task Analysis

ElementAnalysis
Visual typeGrouped bar chart showing changes over time
Main subjectWaste disposal through three different methods
CategoriesLandfill, burning and dumping at sea
Time and unitFour years from 2005 to 2008; million tonnes
Main comparisonChanges in the use of each disposal method and shifts in their rankings
Grouping strategyBP1: contrasting trends in landfill and burning; BP2: sea dumping and final rankings
Main cautionReport quantities, not percentages; use approximations for sea-dumping figures

2. Key Features

5Burning overtook landfill in the final year900 versus 600 million tonnes in 2008

3. Writing Skeleton

Introduction — one sentence

  • Original subject: Waste disposal in one European country in four years.
  • Paraphrased introduction: The bar chart illustrates the average quantity of waste disposed of by landfill, burning and dumping at sea in a European country between 2005 and 2008.

Overview — two sentences

Option A — Recommended

Overall, landfill disposal fell substantially, whereas the amount of waste burned increased steadily and became the highest figure by 2008. By contrast, dumping at sea changed relatively little and finished at its lowest level.

Option B — Alternative

Overall, landfill was by far the dominant method at the beginning, but its use declined markedly over the period. Meanwhile, burning rose consistently and overtook both other methods in the final year, while sea dumping remained broadly stable.

Best choice: Option A
Reason: It clearly presents the three main trends and the most important change in ranking.

TASK 2 – OUTLINE THAM KHẢO

Essay type: Positive or Negative Development
Band target: 6.5–8.0
Topic area: Employment / Equality

Task: In some countries it is now illegal to reject someone applying for a job because of his or her age. Do you think this is a positive or negative development?

1. Task Analysis

ElementDetail
Essay typeA positive-or-negative development essay requiring evaluation rather than a simple list of benefits and drawbacks.
Main topicLaws preventing age discrimination in recruitment.
Keywordsillegal, reject, applying for a job, because of age, positive or negative development.
Task focusEvaluate the effects of preventing employers from rejecting applicants solely on the basis of age.
Required positionA clear overall judgment is essential; this outline argues that the development is largely positive.
Plain-English meaningIs it beneficial or harmful to prohibit employers from refusing applicants simply because they are considered too young or too old?

2. Skeleton

Introduction — 50–70 words

  • General statement: Employment decisions can substantially affect both individual livelihoods and the efficient use of a country’s workforce.
  • Paraphrased task: Several governments have therefore introduced legislation preventing employers from excluding candidates solely because of their age.
  • Thesis: Although such laws may slightly restrict employers when age is genuinely connected to job performance, they are a positive development because they promote merit-based recruitment and allow people of all ages to contribute economically.

Body Paragraph 1 — Potential limitations

PartFunctionSentence / Idea
TSTopic sentenceA possible drawback is that employers may have less freedom to consider age-related limitations in certain occupations.
Expl1Explanation 1Some physically demanding jobs require strength, stamina or rapid reactions that not every older applicant can demonstrate.
ExampleConcrete exampleFor example, firefighters and construction workers must satisfy demanding physical standards to perform their duties safely.
Expl2Explanation 2Employers may also worry that younger candidates lack experience or that older recruits will require costly retraining.
Further dev.DepthHowever, these concerns should be assessed through relevant qualifications, medical checks and performance tests rather than assumptions based on age.
LinkParagraph linkTherefore, the law does not prevent careful recruitment; it merely requires employers to use objective, job-related criteria.

Body Paragraph 2 — Stronger benefits and overall judgment

PartFunctionSentence / Idea
TSTopic sentenceMore importantly, banning age discrimination creates a fairer labour market and enables employers to select the most capable candidates.
Expl1Explanation 1Applicants are judged according to their competence, experience and suitability instead of stereotypes about particular age groups.
ExampleConcrete exampleAn experienced worker in their sixties, for instance, may possess valuable technical knowledge and mentoring skills that a company needs.
Expl2Explanation 2The legislation also helps younger applicants who may otherwise be dismissed as immature despite having strong qualifications and modern skills.
Further dev.DepthIn the long term, wider participation reduces unemployment, strengthens financial independence and helps societies retain useful talent as populations age.
LinkParagraph linkThese lasting social and economic gains clearly outweigh the limited inconvenience imposed on employers.

Conclusion — 40–55 words

  • Restated thesis: Overall, prohibiting employers from rejecting candidates solely because of age is a positive development.
  • Summary: Although age-related abilities may matter in a small number of occupations, objective assessments can address these concerns while preserving equal access to employment.
  • Final thought: Recruitment should ultimately be based on capability rather than age.
